        How to use
        
    
          
      
        • Open one or many files with Open files, or drag them onto the page. Switch between them in the Files list.
          • 
      
        • Rotate: drag with the left mouse button (or one finger).
          • 
      
        • Move: drag with the right mouse button, or hold Shift (two fingers on touch screens).
          • 
      
        • Zoom: mouse wheel or pinch.
          • 
      
        • Inspect: point at a block to see its name and position. Click it to find it in the block list.
          • 
      
        • Layers: use the sliders or the [ ] keys to build up the schematic layer by layer.
          • 
      
        • Hide blocks: untick a block in the list, e.g. to see through glass or remove the ground.
          • 
      
        • Shortcuts: 1–4 camera views, R reset view, P screenshot, F full screen.
          • 
    
        
    
        Blocks are drawn with solid colours that approximate their in-game look. Simple shapes (slabs, stairs, fences, panes, doors, plants…) are shown; complex models are simplified.
